# AI use statement

Stop-GIS may be developed with assistance from generative artificial
intelligence tools. AI-assisted changes remain the responsibility of human
contributors and must be reviewed, tested, and documented to the same standard
as other contributions.

Stop-GIS does not use AI to classify stops or generate research observations by
default. Any project that introduces AI-assisted coding, data transformation,
interpretation, or analysis should:

- validate AI-assisted outputs before using or publishing them;
- preserve appropriate human review and decision-making;
- document the tools and workflow in the project methodology;
- disclose material AI use in resulting publications and datasets; and
- comply with applicable institutional, funder, journal, legal, and ethical
  requirements.

Sensitive, confidential, personal, licensed, or otherwise restricted project
data should not be submitted to an external AI service without the required
authorization, data-processing terms, and security safeguards.

AI-generated content should not be presented as an independent researcher,
reviewer, author, or accountable decision-maker. Human project leads remain
responsible for the integrity, reproducibility, and interpretation of work
produced with Stop-GIS.
